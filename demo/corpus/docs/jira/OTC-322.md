# OTC-322 Roll out rider batching to 30% of Seoul riders

**Type:** Story · **Priority:** Medium · **Status:** Done · **Assignee:** kevin

## Description

Based on the [batching experiment](../notion/rider-batching-experiment), enable `dispatch.batching` for 30% of Seoul riders with the batching radius capped at 600 m (experiment used 800 m).

## Acceptance criteria

- Median delivery time increase under 2 minutes versus control.
- Late delivery rate (> 45 min) under 4.5%.
- Dashboard split by batched / single trips.
